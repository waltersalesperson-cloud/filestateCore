What I Help With

Buyers, investors, and sellers need different support — here's how I help each.

For Buyers

  • Location & feature walkthroughs
  • Payment terms explained clearly
  • Financing & investment potential
  • Document & requirements assistance
  • Reservation paperwork
  • Contracts & coordination with developer, seller, and bank through to completion

For Investors

  • Property & location information
  • Potential use & rental considerations
  • Long-term ownership planning
  • Practical, on-the-ground context
I share information to help you decide — I don't promise guaranteed returns.

For Sellers

  • Buyer sourcing
  • Viewing coordination
  • Offer presentation
  • End-to-end transaction coordination
